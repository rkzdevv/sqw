Three ways to play
Learn

A small window floats over your game and shows the next keys to press.

Wait for me: the song moves only when you press the right key.
Follow the song: keep up with the real tempo, slowed down if you want. (Pro)
Repeat the hard bars, and change the window's size and transparency. (Pro)
Autoplay

Pick a song, click the game window, and SQW plays it with precise timing, chords and tempo changes included.

Playlists: songs play one after another, or one at a time with a key.
Safe: it stops at once if you leave the game or hold Win, Alt or Ctrl.
Any speed, a fixed BPM, or loop one bar or a whole section. (Pro)
Human touch

Bot to Human adds the small timing changes and the occasional slip a real player makes, different every time.

8 levels, from Chaos to Virtuoso.
Play like Beethoven, Mozart, Chopin, Bach or Liszt, or make your own style. (Pro)

How a QWERTY sheet works
f D f D f a d s [6p] t u p [3a] u O a [6s]

Lowercase letters are white keys, capitals add Shift for the black keys, and letters in brackets are played together. This is the opening of Für Elise by Beethoven.

Install
Open Releases on the right side of this page and download SQW-Setup.exe (or SQW-Portable.exe, which runs without installing).
Open it. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The warning appears because SQW is not signed with a paid certificate yet.
Pick SQW Pro or SQW Free and start playing.

You need: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Python comes inside, so there is nothing else to install.

Your data: songs, settings and plugins stay on your computer, in %APPDATA%\SQW. Updating or uninstalling keeps them.

FAQ

Is SQW really free? Yes. RKZ made every edition free for everyone for a limited time. How long is a secret.

Where does Autoplay work? Anywhere that plays piano from the QWERTY keyboard, like virtual piano websites and piano games.

Do I need an account? No. There is no account and no password. SQW works offline.
